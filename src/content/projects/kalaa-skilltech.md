## Why it exists

Plenty of young people in Bihar finish school or college without a skill an employer will pay for. Kalaa SkillTech runs practical, technical training and then connects trainees with real work, so the training ends in a job rather than a certificate.

## What I do

I co-founded it in December 2024, and I build its website. The current version is made for the people we want to enrol: young people in Bihar choosing a trade. It reads in English or Hindi, and it looks like the hand-painted shop signboards they see every day. Type your name and the hero paints your own repair-shop board.

Kalaa Saathi, the AI helper, runs on Gemini through Vercel functions. It suggests a course from a few questions, answers out loud, and looks at a photo of a broken device to say what's likely wrong. It replies in the language you wrote in: English, Hindi or Hinglish.

Every number on the site comes from our pitch deck and company profile, not filler.

## The seed grant

Startup Bihar selected Kalaa SkillTech from more than 10,000 applicants for ₹10 lakh in seed funding.
